Epiphanic Rhythm

   I have begun to wonder what it is that seems to have broken, or perhaps what has finally opened. Have I been called? It is not as if I woke up one day and decided that enough was enough, that I would no longer pretend these terms were acceptable. I never consciously chose this on a whim, never sat down and planned a course of action. And yet, somehow, this feels like the first thing I have ever done in my life that is filled with intention and conscious awareness. When I look back over what I have written or done, I do not feel pride, or even clarity. There is no “Look what I’ve done,” only “What have I done?” The only answer I can arrive at, for once, is the question of why.

   Growing up, I never really knew God. I remember bringing Him up to my parents and hearing the same response every time: “Where is He?” After all, why would God take my sister? When I looked around, there was only loss. Drugs. An older brother in prison. I met the devil long before I met God. Self-limiting beliefs, negativity, vices of every kind, various forms of depravity. I have come to recognize all of these things as expressions of the same force. It was not until around August of last year, 2024, that I felt something equal and opposite to it.

   Around that time, He sat me down and taught me what no one else ever had. How to honor my past self, my present self, and my future self. How to acknowledge my ancestors, my legacy, and my posterity. Somewhere along the way, it was decided that I would wake up each day and learn, grow, and become something more. A light. These realizations only came in hindsight. I wish I could say that I chose, one day, to improve my only life armed with virtues I did not yet possess. That would make no sense. If you had told me I would do any of this, I would have laughed without hesitation. Looking back now, I recognize it as obedience, though it has not felt like that for the past sixteen months.

   I always believed that if I ever heard Him call my name, I would come running. What actually happened looks nothing like what I imagined. The experience has been profound, profoundly humbling, and at times profoundly terrifying. There has been an unknown cost and an unknown return, both still unfolding. I have never known what to expect from any of this, and for the reasons I have already named. Lately, I am beginning to understand that perhaps what I have learned along the way is the thing to be grateful for. The transformation itself is the destination.
